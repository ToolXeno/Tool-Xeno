Welcome to Tool Xeno. We believe your personal data belongs to you — and only you. That belief shaped every decision we made while building this platform. This policy explains, in plain language, how we handle (or rather, don't handle) your information.

🔒 The Core Principle: Your Data Stays with You

Every tool on Tool Xeno runs 100% inside your browser. Your files, images, documents, and text are processed locally on your device. Nothing gets uploaded to our servers — ever.

When you crop an image, compress a PDF, or convert a file, the entire operation happens on your computer or phone. We can't see your files, we don't store them, and we have no way of accessing them. This isn't just a policy — it's built into how our tools fundamentally work.

🚫 What We Never Collect

Because all processing happens on your device, we never collect, view, or store:

  • The content of any files you work with — images, documents, text, or otherwise
  • Any personal information embedded in or associated with your files
  • Results generated by our tools — cropped images, converted files, compressed documents
  • Your browsing behavior on specific tools or the data you input into them
📊 The Limited Information We Collect

To keep improving Tool Xeno and ensure it works well for everyone, we collect a small amount of anonymous, non-personal information:

  • Usage analytics: Which tools are popular, general traffic patterns, and how people navigate the site. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.
  • Device and browser info: Basic details like browser type and operating system — used solely to make sure the site works correctly across different devices.

That's it. Nothing more.

🍪 Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage for one reason only: to remember whether you prefer the light or dark theme. This is stored on your device and is not a tracking cookie of any kind.

Our third-party advertising partners may use their own cookies to serve relevant ads. These are managed entirely by the ad networks and are not controlled by or shared with us. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your ad provider's settings (for example, Google Ad Settings).

📢 About Advertisements

We display ads through third-party networks (such as Google AdSense) to keep all our tools completely free for everyone. Here's what you should know:

  • Ad partners may collect standard information like IP addresses and general geolocation to show relevant ads
  • We never share any personal data or file content with these ad networks — because we don't have that data in the first place
  • You can control personalized ad preferences through your ad provider's settings
🛡️ Data Security

The strongest security is not having data to protect. Since we never receive or store your files, the risk of a data breach involving your content simply doesn't exist on our end.

All processing is handled within your browser's sandboxed environment — one of the most secure computing environments available. Your files remain on your device from start to finish.

👶 Children's Privacy

Our tools are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone in that age group. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal data, please contact us and we'll take immediate action.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we'll post the revised version here with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page occasionally so you're always informed about how we protect your privacy.
