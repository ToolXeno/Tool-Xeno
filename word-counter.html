FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The word counter splits your text by whitespace characters (spaces, tabs, newlines) and counts each non-empty segment as one word. It intelligently handles multiple consecutive spaces, leading and trailing whitespace, and mixed line breaks without counting empty gaps as words.
Characters with spaces counts every single character in your text, including letters, numbers, punctuation, spaces, tabs, and line breaks. Characters without spaces subtracts all whitespace characters (spaces, tabs, newlines, and other Unicode whitespace) from the total count.

There is no enforced character limit. The counter can handle very large texts comprising hundreds of thousands of characters smoothly. The JavaScript engine is optimized for performance, so even lengthy documents are processed without lag or delay.
Sentences are detected by looking for common sentence-ending punctuation marks (period, exclamation mark, question mark) followed by whitespace or the end of text. Paragraphs are identified by one or more consecutive line breaks that separate blocks of text.
